# Brief - Estate of Cavanaugh v. Andrade (negligence)

**Facts**

* Andrade was approaching an intersection when he observed Zergoski pass several stopped vehicles and speed through a solid red traffic signal controlling the intersection. Andrade turned at the intersection and increased his speed to follow Zergoski.
* Andrade activated his emergency lights and siren approximately two blocks later, which caused Zergoski to begin to pull over; Zergoski again <mark style="background-color:orange;">fled at a high rate of speed</mark> and turned onto 39th Street. Approximately six to seven blocks later, Andrade observed <mark style="background-color:orange;">Zergoski's vehicle stalled in an intersection. Andrade was able to approach close enough to observe Zergoski's license plate number, which he relayed to the dispatcher. Zergoski restarted his vehicle and again fled at a high rate of speed along West Morgan Avenue, which is a main arterial street within a primarily residential neighborhood.</mark>
* At the intersection of 60th Street and Morgan, <mark style="background-color:orange;">Zergoski went through another red light and collided with a vehicle driven by Donald Cavanaugh</mark>. According to witnesses, <mark style="background-color:orange;">Zergoski was \[traveling] between 60 and 80 miles per hour approximately two blocks prior</mark> to this intersection. <mark style="background-color:green;">Andrade estimated that he was approximately one block away from this collision when it occurred</mark>. A w<mark style="background-color:orange;">itness testified that Andrade was within approximately one-half block of Zergoski's vehicle when it entered the intersection</mark>. In all, the pursuit spanned approximately 17 blocks.
* &#x20;As a result of the collision, <mark style="background-color:orange;">Cavanaugh died after spending five months in a coma</mark>.
* James Cavanaugh, Donald's father, sued the City, Andrade, and Zergoski, individually and as administrator of Donald's estate. The case was ultimately tried to a <mark style="background-color:orange;">jury, which found the City 23% negligent with respect to implementation of its high-speed pursuit policy, Andrade 2% negligent with respect to the operation of his vehicle,</mark> and Zergoski 75% negligent.

**Issue**

Whether the city was negligent in carrying out its ministerial duty
